Explore Malvan

Malvan is known for its beaches, a sea fort and its own coastal cuisine. Ask us at the guest house for the best way to plan each day.

Tarkarli Beach

Coastal views and water activities in the Tarkarli area.

Sindhudurg Fort

A historic sea fort reached by boat from Malvan jetty.

Rock Garden

A seaside rocky garden and viewpoint in Malvan town.

Malvani food

Discover local coastal cuisine and Malvani flavours.

Dandi Beach
